# Sales Analytics Insights

## Dataset Overview

- **Orders:** 970 transactions across full year 2024
- **Customers:** 80 unique customers
- **Total Revenue:** $430,658
- **Total Profit:** $201,787
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 46.9%

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Trends
- Q4 (October–November) recorded the strongest sales volume, with November peaking at ~$57K — consistent with end-of-year procurement cycles
- Q3 (June–July) was the weakest period, dipping below $25K in both months, suggesting a seasonal lull that the business could target with mid-year promotions
- Month-over-month growth was volatile, indicating no consistent growth trajectory — revenue is largely event-driven rather than compounding

### Product Performance
- **Laptop Pro** was the single largest revenue driver, accounting for approximately 45% of total revenue ($196K) — despite having a lower order frequency than smaller items
- **Standing Desk** and **Office Chair** ranked 2nd and 3rd respectively, confirming that Furniture is a high-value category even with fewer units sold
- **Notebook Pack** and **Pen Set** had the highest order volumes but contributed minimally to revenue — high velocity, low value products

### Regional Performance
- **North** led all regions in revenue (~$139K), followed by **East** (~$109K) and **South** (~$106K)
- **West** significantly underperformed at ~$76K, representing only 17.7% of total revenue despite likely having comparable market size
- Average order value was similar across regions, suggesting the West gap is a volume/reach problem, not a pricing problem

### Profit Margins
- **Electronics** delivered the highest absolute profit due to volume and pricing power on flagship products
- **Stationery** had the thinnest margins, driven by low unit prices and bulk-buy discounting
- **Furniture** showed strong margins on premium items (Standing Desk, Office Chair) — a strategic category to grow

### Customer Segments
- **SMB** customers generated the most orders and revenue in aggregate, as expected from the largest segment
- **Enterprise** customers had the highest revenue-per-customer ratio, making them high-value targets for retention and upselling
- **Retail** customers were the most price-sensitive, with lower average order values

---

## Recommendations

1. **Protect and grow the Electronics category** — Laptop Pro alone drives nearly half of revenue. Ensuring consistent stock, competitive pricing, and upsell bundles (monitor + keyboard + headset) could meaningfully increase basket size.

2. **Investigate the West region** — The revenue gap between West and other regions is too large to ignore. A targeted regional campaign or dedicated sales rep allocation could recover significant revenue.

3. **Address the Q3 dip** — June and July underperformed by 30–40% compared to peak months. A mid-year promotion or customer re-engagement campaign timed to June could smooth the revenue curve.

4. **Focus retention efforts on Enterprise customers** — They purchase less frequently but spend more per order. A dedicated account management approach and loyalty incentives would maximise lifetime value.

5. **Reassess Stationery pricing** — High volume but thin margins suggest either a repricing opportunity or a bundling strategy to raise average transaction value.
