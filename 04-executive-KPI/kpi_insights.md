# Executive KPI Dashboard Insights

## Dataset Overview

- **Period:** January 2022 – December 2024 (36 months)
- **Business Type:** B2B subscription / services company
- **Metrics Tracked:** Revenue, Gross Profit, Net Profit, Customer Growth, Marketing Efficiency

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Growth
- Total revenue grew from **$3.21M (2022)** to **$3.67M (2023)** to **$4.53M (2024)**
- Year-over-year growth accelerated: **+14.2% in 2023**, then **+23.4% in 2024** — a positive growth inflection
- Q4 consistently outperforms other quarters across all three years, confirming a strong seasonal procurement cycle
- Q1 is the weakest quarter every year — a predictable lull that should be offset with mid-year pipeline activity

### Profitability
- Net profit margin held steady at **33–35%** across all three years, showing that growth did not come at the expense of profitability
- Gross margins were consistent at approximately 58–62%, suggesting stable cost of delivery
- 2024 net profit reached **$1.55M** — the strongest year on record, up 25% from 2022

### Customer Growth
- Active customer base grew steadily year-on-year
- New customer acquisition increased in 2024, reflecting either expanded marketing investment or improved conversion
- Monthly churn remained within a manageable band; no single month showed a spike that would indicate a systemic retention issue

### Marketing Efficiency
- Marketing spend as a percentage of revenue stayed within the 6–10% band
- Cost per acquisition (CPA) trended favourably as customer volume increased — a sign of improving marketing efficiency at scale
- 2024 showed the best CPA ratio, meaning more customers were acquired per dollar spent than in prior years

### Seasonality Patterns
- The 3-month rolling average smooths the seasonal volatility and shows a consistent upward trajectory
- November and December are reliably the strongest months; January and February are the softest
- Same-month comparisons across years confirm that every month in 2024 outperformed its 2023 equivalent

---

## Recommendations

1. **Double down on Q4 pipeline** — It consistently delivers the highest revenue. Allocate additional sales and marketing resources starting in September to maximise Q4 conversion.

2. **Address Q1 revenue softness** — The recurring January dip suggests customers defer decisions at year start. A Q1 incentive programme (early renewal discounts, bundled offers) could meaningfully smooth the revenue curve.

3. **Protect net margin as revenue scales** — Margins have held steady through rapid growth, which is excellent. As the business targets further expansion, monitor operating expense growth to ensure it stays below revenue growth.

4. **Invest in retention alongside acquisition** — Customer growth is strong, but the most capital-efficient path at this stage is reducing churn. Even a 1–2 percentage point improvement in monthly churn rate would compound significantly over 12 months.

5. **Use YoY same-month comparisons for board reporting** — The month-by-month YoY analysis (Q5 query) is the most executive-relevant view. It controls for seasonality and immediately shows whether the business is growing or contracting on a like-for-like basis.
