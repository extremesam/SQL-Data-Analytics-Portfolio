# SQL Data Analytics Portfolio

## 👋 About This Portfolio

This repository showcases a collection of **real-world business analytics projects built using SQL**, demonstrating the ability to transform raw data into **actionable business insights and executive-level reporting**.

Each project simulates a real business domain and focuses on solving practical problems such as:

* Employee attrition and workforce analytics
* Revenue and sales performance analysis
* Customer churn prediction and retention insights
* Executive KPI and business performance tracking

The goal is not just SQL execution, but **business decision-making through data**.

---

## 📊 Featured Projects

### 🟢 01 — HR Analytics (People Analytics)

Analyzes employee data to uncover patterns in attrition, salary distribution, and performance metrics.

**Key Insights:**

* Department-level attrition analysis
* Salary vs performance relationships
* Workforce risk segmentation

**Skills Used:**

* Aggregations
* CASE statements
* Window functions
* Group-based analysis

---

### 🔵 02 — Sales Analytics (Revenue Intelligence)

Analyzes 970 sales transactions across 2024 to identify revenue drivers, regional performance, and product profitability.

**Key Insights:**

* Top-performing products (Laptop Pro dominates revenue)
* Regional performance gaps (West underperforming)
* Seasonal revenue trends (Q4 peak performance)

**Skills Used:**

* Time-series analysis
* CTEs
* Window functions (RANK, LAG)
* Revenue and profit analysis

---

### 🔴 03 — Customer Churn Analysis (Retention Analytics)

Analyzes telecom customers to understand churn behavior and identify high-risk segments.

**Key Insights:**

* 34.4% overall churn rate
* Month-to-month contracts have highest churn (~50%)
* Early tenure customers are most at risk

**Skills Used:**

* Cohort-style analysis
* CASE-based segmentation
* Subqueries and CTEs
* Risk scoring logic

---

### 🟣 04 — Executive KPI Dashboard (Business Intelligence)

A 36-month business performance system simulating executive-level reporting.

**Key Insights:**

* Revenue growth: +14.2% (2023), +23.4% (2024)
* Stable profit margins (33–35%)
* Strong seasonal Q4 performance
* Marketing efficiency variability identified

**Skills Used:**

* LAG / LEAD window functions
* Rolling averages
* YoY and MoM analysis
* Executive KPI modeling
